<h1>FashionShop App 🛍️</h1>
FashionShop is an innovative Android application designed to revolutionize the online shopping experience with its user-friendly interface and comprehensive features.

<h2>Tools Used:</h2>
  <li>Programming Language: Kotlin</li>
  <li>Android SDK</li>
  <li>Libraries: Kotlin Coroutines (Flow), Retrofit, Glide, Material Design, Jetpack (Navigation Component, ViewModel), View Binding, Gson, Lottie.</li>
  <li>Backend: Shopify API</li>
  <li>Authentication: Firebase Authentication</li>
  <li>Design Patterns: Builder, Factory, Observer, Singleton</li>
  <li>Unit Testing: Ensured robustness with unit tests.</li>
  <li>Persistence: Shared Preferences for local storage.</li>

<h2>Key Features</h2>
  <li>🏡 Intuitive Home Design: Navigate through a clean and organized home screen for a seamless shopping experience.</li>
  <li>🏷️ Special Offers: Apply discount codes to save on purchases.</li>
  <li>🔍 Explore Products: Discover products by browsing through different brands and categories. Easily manage and track orders.</li>
  <li>🗂️ Categories: Explore products under Women, Men, Kids, and Sale sections.</li>
  <li>☢Product Types: Discover a variety of products including T-Shirts, Shoes, and Accessories.</li>
  <li>🛒 Shopping Cart and Payment: Add, remove, and adjust item quantities. Choose from various payment options including Visa or cash.</li>
  <li>❤️Favorites and Search: Save favorite products and use the search bar for quick access.</li>
  <li>⚙️ Customizable Settings: Change currency, manage addresses, and switch languages.</li>
  <li>📝 Detailed Product Info: View descriptions, images, reviews, and ratings.</li>
  <li>🔒 Secure Authentication: Protect accounts with secure login and authentication.</li>
  <li>👤 Guest Access: Browse products and explore features without creating an account.</li>

<h2>Project Management</h2>
   <li>Architectural Pattern: MVVM (Model-View-ViewModel) architecture.</li>
   <li>Tools Used: Trello for task management and GitHub for version control.</li>
